\begin{frame}{Test Problem: Lorenz96}
We test the Lorenz96 ODE:
$$\dot{x}_i = (x_{i+1} - x_{i-2})x_{i-1} - x_i + F,$$
and take the initial condition randomly generated in the cube $[-5, 5]^d$, and randomly generate $F \in [1,5]$.
